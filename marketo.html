From marketing lead to sales follow-up.

Connecting selected APAC leads to the global Salesforce workflow, with consistent data and a clear next step for sales.

PRODUCT MISSION

Help regional marketing leads become actionable follow-up for global sales teams.

Problem & goal

Pharmaceutical leads from APAC needed to be available in both the regional system and the global Salesforce organization for follow-up and revenue tracking. The standard Marketo sync already existed, but did not cover this particular cross-region workflow.

The goal: automate the missing connection while preserving the global team’s existing business rules.

Solution

A targeted integration through IBM Integration Bus and Salesforce REST APIs. It validates and maps incoming data, attaches a follow-up task to an existing contact, or creates a new lead and task when needed.

User journey

My role

I defined data contracts and built the Salesforce REST API used by the integration. My work covered field mapping, required-field validation, lead-processing logic, and error logging.

Decisions

Extend the established sales workflow.

The incoming leads needed to fit the way the global team already worked. We retained those business rules and added a targeted regional connection.

Agree on the data before moving it.

Field mappings and required values became an explicit contract between systems. Validating the payload at the boundary helped keep incompatible or incomplete records out of the workflow.

Give failed records a clear next step.

Error logging and explicit success or failure responses helped the teams identify records requiring correction and reprocessing.

Impact

The integration automated a previously manual path for selected regional leads and supported Salesforce workflows serving 400+ sales representatives. Follow-up could use the global team’s expected records and tasks.

400+ describes the sales population served, rather than measured adoption or a conversion increase.

How I would measure the handoff

Track valid leads arriving successfully, time from marketing submission to a sales task, duplicate creation, and unresolved integration errors. These are proposed measures; no conversion lift is claimed.

What I took away

A useful integration carries the meaning of the workflow along with the data: what counts as a valid lead, when to reuse a record, and who needs to act next.
